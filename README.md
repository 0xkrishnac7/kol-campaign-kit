# kol campaign kit 📣

free, copy-paste templates for running KOL / influencer campaigns in crypto, fintech and AI.

most KOL campaigns get judged on views and likes. this kit is built to judge them on what actually matters: sign-ups, funded accounts, and users who stick around.

> rule #1: judge a KOL by the users they bring, not the reach you buy.

## what's inside

| # | file | use it when |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [vetting checklist](templates/01-vetting-checklist.md) | before you shortlist anyone |
| 02 | [outreach DMs](templates/02-outreach-dms.md) | first message, follow-up, and the "no thanks" |
| 03 | [campaign brief](templates/03-kol-brief.md) | once a creator says yes |
| 04 | [tracker](templates/04-tracker.csv) | through the campaign, one row per creator per post |
| 05 | [post-campaign report](templates/05-post-campaign-report.md) | 2 weeks after the last post goes live |

## how to use
1. vet with **01**, reach out with **02**
2. send **03** the moment a creator agrees. short brief, clear deliverables, clear payment terms
3. give every creator their own tracked link and log everything in **04**
4. two weeks after wrap-up, fill in **05** and decide who you work with again

## principles
- creators who actually use the product > big accounts who don't
- a series (3-4 pieces over a month) > one paid post
- every creator gets a tracked link. if you can't attribute it, you can't scale it
- always disclose paid partnerships. your brand's trust is on the line too
- check local rules on financial promotions before you brief anyone on a crypto product
