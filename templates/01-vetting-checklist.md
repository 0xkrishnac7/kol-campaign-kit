# 01 · KOL vetting checklist

run this before anyone goes on your shortlist. 10 minutes per creator saves you a wasted budget.

## audience
- [ ] do the comments have real questions from real people, or just "🔥🔥 great project sir"?
- [ ] does engagement look normal for their size? (sudden spikes on random posts = red flag)
- [ ] is the audience in the region / language you're targeting?
- [ ] do followers look like your users (traders, builders, students, normies)?

## content
- [ ] do they explain things, or just drop links with 🚀?
- [ ] have they used products like yours, on camera or in screenshots?
- [ ] what do their last 5 paid posts look like? did they disclose them?
- [ ] do they post in the format you need (threads, shorts, long video, spaces)?

## reputation
- [ ] search their handle + "scam", "rug", "paid". anything you'd be embarrassed by?
- [ ] have they promoted projects that went to zero or rugged? how did they handle it?
- [ ] do other founders / marketers you trust vouch for them?

## fit
- [ ] would your best users already follow this person?
- [ ] can they talk about your product honestly, including the downsides?
- [ ] are they open to a series, not just one post?

**score it:** 10+ ticks = shortlist · 7-9 = maybe, test with a small first post · under 7 = skip
