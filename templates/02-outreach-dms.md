# 02 · outreach DMs

short, specific, no "dear sir/ma'am". fill in the `[brackets]`.

## first message
> hey [name], loved your [specific post / video] on [topic], especially [one specific thing]. 
>
> i run marketing at [project]. we [one line on what the product does]. 
>
> we're looking for 3-4 creators to do a short series over [timeframe], paid. your audience feels like a great fit because [reason]. 
>
> open to a quick chat? happy to share the brief first.

## follow-up (after 3-4 days, once only)
> hey [name], bumping this in case it got buried. no pressure either way, would just love to know if it's a fit 🙏

## when they ask "what's the budget?"
> for a [number]-piece series over [timeframe], we're thinking [range]. open to your rate card too. we care more about fit and a real walkthrough than one big post.

## when you're passing
> thanks for getting back, [name]. we're going a different direction for this campaign, but i'd love to keep you in mind for the next one. really appreciate your time.

## when they're a no
> totally get it, thanks for letting me know. if anything changes, my DMs are open 🙌

**rules**
- personalise the first line every time. if you can't, you haven't looked at their content yet
- never send the same DM to 50 people at once
- put everything agreed in writing before any money moves
